# Billing and Refunds

## B01：Do you offer refunds?

> On annual plans, you can get a full refund within 30 days of the first purchase or a renewal. After 30 days, we can turn off auto-renew, but we don't refund the rest of the year. Monthly plans aren't refunded for partial months; you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

**Follow-up: How long does a refund take?**
> Once approved, refunds go back to the original card in 5 to 10 business days. The timing after that depends on the customer's bank.

## B02：Why was I charged twice?

> Most of the time, one of the two is a pending authorization from a card check, not a real charge. It drops off on its own within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges show as completed on the card statement, collect the two transaction dates and amounts and escalate to billing. Don't promise a refund before billing confirms.

## B03：Why is my bill higher than last month?

> The usual reasons are new seats added during the month, which are charged prorated, or a switch from annual to monthly pricing. The invoice in Settings, Billing, Invoices lists every seat change with its date.

## B04：How can I pay?

> We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express on all plans. Annual plans over $3,000 can also pay by invoice with bank transfer, with 30-day payment terms.

## B05：Where can I find my invoices?

> Workspace owners and billing admins can download every invoice as a PDF from Settings, Billing, Invoices. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact each time we charge.

## B06：Can I change the company name or VAT number on my invoice?

> Yes. Update it in Settings, Billing, Billing details. Changes apply to future invoices. For a past invoice to be reissued, escalate to billing with the invoice number.

## B07：My card was declined. What happens now?

> We retry the card three times over 7 days and email the billing contact each time. The workspace stays fully active during that time. If all retries fail, the workspace switches to read-only until a working card is added. Nothing is deleted.

## B08：Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?

> Yes, anytime from Settings, Billing. We charge the annual price right away and credit the unused part of the current month.

**Follow-up: Can I switch from annual to monthly?**
> Yes, but the change happens at the end of the current annual term, not immediately.

## B09：Do you charge sales tax or VAT?

> We charge sales tax or VAT where the law requires it, based on the billing address. Businesses in the EU and UK can add a valid VAT number to have VAT reverse-charged.
