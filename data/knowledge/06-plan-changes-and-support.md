# Plan Changes, Cancellation and Support

## C01：How do I cancel my subscription or account?

> The workspace owner can cancel in Settings, Billing, Cancel subscription. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then the workspace moves to read-only for 90 days.

**Follow-up: Can I pause my subscription instead?**
> We don't have a pause option. The closest thing is downgrading to Free, which keeps the data but limits the workspace to 5 members and 3 active projects.

## C02：Can you give me a retention discount or lower price to stay?

> Agents can't offer retention discounts. If the customer is thinking about cancelling because of price, acknowledge it and offer to connect them with their account manager, who can review options.

## C03：Can I downgrade from Business to Team?

> Yes. The downgrade takes effect at the next renewal. Business features like SSO and the audit log switch off at that point, so admins should make sure everyone has a password or another way to log in first.

## C04：Is there a free trial?

> Yes. New workspaces get a 14-day trial of Business. No credit card is needed. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to Free unless someone picks a paid plan.

**Follow-up: Can I extend my trial?**
> Support can extend a trial once by 7 days. Anything longer needs approval from sales.

## C05：What are your support hours?

> Chat and email support are available 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, on every paid plan. Phone support is for Business and Enterprise, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the customer's region on weekdays. Free workspaces get email support and the help center.

## C06：How fast do you reply to support tickets?

> The first-response target is 4 business hours on Business and Enterprise, 1 business day on Team, and 2 business days on Free. Urgent outages for Enterprise are answered within 1 hour, 24/7.
