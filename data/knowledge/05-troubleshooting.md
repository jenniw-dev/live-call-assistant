# Troubleshooting

## T01：I can't log in. What should I do?

> First check if their workspace uses SSO. If it does, they need to use "Log in with SSO" and their company email, and their IT team controls access. If not, have them reset their password from the login page. If they're stuck in a login loop, ask them to clear cookies for pebblework.app or try a private window.

## T02：My teammate didn't get the invite email.

> Ask them to check spam and any "Promotions" tab first. Invite emails come from no-reply@pebblework.app, so their IT team may need to allow that address. An admin can also copy the invite link from Settings, Members, and send it directly. Invites expire after 7 days.

## T03：Slack notifications stopped working.

> The most common cause is that the person who connected Slack left the company or lost admin rights in Slack. Have an admin reconnect it in Settings, Integrations, Slack. Then check that the project still has a channel selected.

## T04：Google Calendar isn't syncing.

> Have the user disconnect and reconnect Google Calendar in Profile, Integrations. Sync only includes tasks that have a due date and are assigned to them. Changes can take up to 15 minutes to appear.

## T05：My CSV import failed.

> Check three things: the file is saved as UTF-8, it's under 10 MB, and it has 5,000 rows or fewer. The first row must be column headers. If it still fails, ask the customer to send the file to support so the import team can look at it.

## T06：Pebblework is slow for me.

> Ask if it's slow everywhere or in one big project. For one big project, switching from Board to List view usually helps, and archiving finished tasks reduces load. If it's slow everywhere, check status.pebblework.app for an incident, then ask them to try another browser to rule out extensions.

## T07：I'm not getting push notifications on my phone.

> Ask them to check that notifications are allowed for Pebblework in the phone's settings, and that "Push" is on in Profile, Notifications in the app. Logging out and back in on the phone refreshes the device registration.

## T08：Is Pebblework down right now?

> Check status.pebblework.app. It shows live status and any ongoing incident. If there's an incident, share the status page link and say updates are posted there every 30 minutes.
