# Plans and Pricing

> Pebblework is a fictional project management product. All prices, policies and names in this knowledge base are invented for the demo.

## P01：What plans do you have?

> We have four plans. Free is for up to 5 people. Team is $9 per user per month on annual billing, or $11 month to month. Business is $19 per user per month annual, or $23 monthly, and adds SSO, audit logs and phone support. Enterprise is custom pricing for larger companies that need things like EU data residency or SCIM.

**Follow-up: Which plan do most teams pick?**
> Most teams of 10 to 50 people start on Team. They usually move to Business when IT asks for SSO or an audit log.

## P02：How much does the Team plan cost?

> Team is $9 per user per month if you pay yearly, or $11 per user per month if you pay monthly. There's no minimum number of seats.

## P03：How much does the Business plan cost?

> Business is $19 per user per month on annual billing, or $23 per user per month on monthly billing.

## P04：What's the difference between Team and Business, and what does Business include that Team doesn't?

> Team gives you unlimited projects, 50 GB of storage per workspace, guests, integrations and the API. Business adds SAML single sign-on, an audit log, custom admin roles, 500 GB of storage, a 99.9% uptime SLA and phone support.

**Follow-up: Is SSO available on Team?**
> No, SSO starts on Business. Two-factor authentication is available on every plan, including Free.

## P05：Is there a free plan or free version, and what's included?

> Free covers up to 5 members, 3 active projects and 1 GB of storage. You get the board, list and calendar views, plus the mobile apps. Integrations, guests and the API are on paid plans.

## P06：Do you have a discount for annual billing?

> Yes. Paying yearly works out about 18 percent cheaper than paying month to month. For example, Team is $9 per user per month on annual versus $11 on monthly.

## P07：Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

> Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50 percent off Team or Business. The customer applies through the discount form in Settings, and it usually takes 2 to 3 business days to approve.

**Follow-up: Can you give me another discount on top of that?**
> Agents can't stack or promise extra discounts. If the customer is asking for more, offer to connect them with the sales team.

## P08：How does Enterprise pricing work?

> Enterprise is quoted by the sales team. It usually starts at 100 seats and includes everything in Business plus SCIM provisioning, EU data residency, a dedicated customer success manager and a custom contract.

## P09：What happens if I add more users in the middle of my billing cycle?

> New seats are charged right away, prorated for the time left in your billing period. So if you add someone halfway through the year, you pay for half a year for that seat.

## P10：What happens if I remove users?

> Removing a user frees the seat immediately, but we don't refund the unused time. The lower seat count applies from your next renewal. If you add someone back before then, you can reuse the paid seat at no extra cost.

## P11：Do guests count as paid seats?

> No. Guests are free on Team and Business. Each workspace can invite up to 5 guests per paid member. Guests can only see the projects they're invited to.

## P12：Which currencies can I pay in?

> We bill in US dollars, euros or British pounds. The currency is set when you first subscribe and can't be changed on the same subscription.
